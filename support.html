Pocket Pals — Support

Pocket Pals is a texting practice app with pretend pals. Messages are never sent to real people. Most of the app runs on your device. Optional family sync keeps the grown-up’s setup in a shared Apple iCloud (CloudKit) database run by the developer, not in your own iCloud account. The Privacy Policy explains what is stored.

For help, email brighamr@gmail.com.
